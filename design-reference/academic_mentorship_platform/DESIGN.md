## Brand & Style

The visual narrative bridges classical academic rigor with high-momentum modern mentorship. Designed for ambitious university students and emerging professionals, the interface establishes an atmosphere of prestige, deliberate pacing, and intellectual authority without sliding into institutional stiffness.

Drawing from modern editorial design and refined typography, the aesthetic uses warm cream paper-like backdrops, sharp literary serif display titles, and precision-engineered sans-serif interface elements. Whitespace is generous and architectural, reinforcing focus, mental clarity, and high-value mentorship engagements.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authentic, warm, and intellectual tone through carefully balanced values:

- **Key CTA & Energy (`#C4542D`):** A warm, earthen terracotta seed tone acting as the decisive point of action, focus states, and career-advancement highlights. Hover states transition deeper into `#A84220`.
- **Institutional Core (`#101D33`):** Deep navy acts as the foundation of textual hierarchy, high-contrast surfaces, and grounding brand elements.
- **Atmospheric Neutral (`#FAF8F3`):** Warm cream provides paper-like reading warmth for page canvases, softening contrast against pure white cards.
- **Editorial Slate (`#64748B`):** Balances meta-information, secondary descriptions, and architectural lines without overpowering the typography.
- **Structural Outlines (`#E2E8F0`):** Ultra-fine neutral boundaries keeping dense student portals uncluttered.

## Typography

The type system merges the literary gravitas of `Newsreader` with the contemporary functional clarity of `Manrope`.

- **Editorial Serifs (`Newsreader`):** Applied exclusively to hero headers, syllabus section titles, case study vignettes, and milestone badges. It evokes masterclasses, published scholarship, and institutional authority.
- **Instrument Sans (`Manrope`):** Carries all interactive components, curriculum trackers, inputs, and analytical labels.
- **Micro-Copy & Eyebrows:** Handled by `label-md` and `label-sm` in all-caps tracking with positive letter spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to act as refined editorial categorizers above titles.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured 12-column grid system anchored to a max-width container of `1280px` on desktop viewports. The spacing architecture maintains a calm rhythm:

- **Mobile (&lt; 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` gutters, and `1.5rem` canvas margins. Stacked cards and single-column mentorship lists.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` gutters, and `3rem` canvas margins. 2-column modules for skill tracks and mentor bios.
- **Desktop (&gt; 1024px):** 12-column layout, `2rem` gutters, and `5rem` canvas margins. Asymmetrical 8:4 splits pairing deep curriculum roadmaps with sticky career progress bars.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy prioritizes tactile paper layering over artificial digital glow. Surfaces stay crisp and level:

- **Surface Tiers:** The background canvas relies on Warm Cream (`#FAF8F3`). Interactive modules, cohort dossiers, and assessment hubs elevate directly onto stark White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Low-Contrast Structural Borders:** Outlines are consistent: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. They delineate data blocks cleanly in lieu of heavy shadow treatment.
- **Ambient Academic Shadows:** When layering requires separation (e.g., dropdowns, sticky action bars, mentor preview cards), use warm deep-navy tinting:
  - *Resting Card:* `0 1px 3px rgba(16, 29, 51, 0.04), 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(16, 29, 51, 0.05)`
  - *Hover/Active Card:* `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(16, 29, 51, 0.08)`
  - *Overlays & Drawers:* `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(16, 29, 51, 0.12)`

## Shapes

The interface embraces a restrained geometric language (`roundedness: 1`). Controls, inputs, and primary containers maintain soft-squared contours (0.25rem base radius; cards scale between 0.5rem and 0.75rem / `12px–16px`). This avoids bubbly casualness while softening strict modernist rectangularity, preserving an authoritative, tailored profile.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Terracotta (`#C4542D`) background with crisp white typography (`Manrope`, 600 weight). Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Corner radius: `0.5rem`. Hover state shifts smoothly to `#A84220`.
- **Secondary (Institutional):** Solid Deep Navy (`#101D33`) background with white typography. Used for portal logins, final submissions, and syllabus certifications.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** White or transparent fill, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, Deep Navy text. Hover applies `#FAF8F3` background with border tinting to `#CBD5E1`.

### Badges & Metadata Chips
- Small, uppercase tracking (`0.06em`), `label-md` typographic profile.
- Backgrounds leverage tonal tints: Cream tint (`#FAF8F3`) with Slate text (`#64748B`), or Terracotta soft tint (`#FDF2ED`) with Terracotta text (`#C4542D`).
- Radii are contained at `0.25rem` to match the structured academic framing.

### Cards (Coursework & Mentorship)
- Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) surface, framed with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`).
- Border-radius: `0.75rem` (12px).
- Internal padding: `1.5rem` (`space-lg`).
- Header metadata integrates Newsreader titles directly with track tags and estimated module timelines. Hover transitions smoothly elevate via the ambient academic shadow.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `0.75rem 1rem` padding, `0.375rem` radius. Placeholder text in `#94A3B8`. Focus state applies a crisp `1px solid #C4542D` border with a subtle 2px Terracotta ring at `15%` opacity.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Deep Navy border in resting state; filled Terracotta (`#C4542D`) with white interior check/dot when selected.
- **Lists & Milestones:** Connected by vertical 1px hairline rules (`#E2E8F0`) with numeric milestone indicators set in `Newsreader` italics.
