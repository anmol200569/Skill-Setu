In the Raft consensus algorithm, what condition must be strictly fulfilled before a cluster Leader can commit an entry from its log to its localized state machine?

According to Raft paper §5.3, a leader commits a log entry once it is replicated across a majority (quorum) of servers. Furthermore, Raft mandates that the leader only commits an entry from its current term by counting replicas, preventing uncommitted overwrite anomalies during leader replacement.
